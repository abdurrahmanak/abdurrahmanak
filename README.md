### What I do

I design and lead the engineering of an **AI-powered business process platform** — a system that unifies customer discovery, purchase-intent scoring, omnichannel communication, CRM and operations tracking under a single AI layer.

It does not only report. It acts: follow-ups, alerts and automations are triggered by the system itself.

I own the whole stack of decisions: system architecture, the AI layer, infrastructure, security standards and the technology roadmap.

---

### How I work

- **Security is day one**, not a layer added before launch. OWASP-driven design, tenant isolation, auth architecture.
- **If I can't measure it, I don't claim it.** I raised multilingual AI response accuracy from 85% to 100% — behind that number is an evaluation harness I built.
- **Production scars beat theory.** Most of my architectural decisions come from having watched a design break under real load.
